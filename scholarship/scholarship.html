About RPST

What is RPST 2027?

RANJAN'S PHYSICS SCHOLARSHIP TEST (RPST) 2027 is a scholarship-focused academic test created to provide students an opportunity to demonstrate their preparation and compete for scholarship benefits.

The test is available in both online and offline modes and is organised across two phases.
