Unit-wise Syllabus

CLASS 10 SCIENCE

Unit I · ChemistryChemical Substances – Nature and Behaviour

  • Chemical Reactions and Equations
  • Acids, Bases, and Salts
  • Metals and Non-metals
  • Carbon and Its Compounds (Preparation, Properties, Uses)

Unit II · BiologyThe World of the Living

  • Life Processes
  • Control and Coordination (in Plants and Animals)
  • Reproduction
  • Heredity

Unit III · PhysicsLight

  • Reflection of Light
  • Refraction of Light
  • Lenses
  • Human Eye and Optical Phenomena
  • Optical Effects

Unit IV · PhysicsEffects of Current

  • Electricity
  • Electric Circuits
  • Heating Effect of Current
  • Electric Power
  • Magnetic Effects of Current
  • AC and DC
  • Domestic Electric Circuits

Unit V · Environmental ScienceNatural Resources

  • Our Environment
  • Waste Management
Practical Work · Experiments Included
  • Properties of acids and bases: litmus test, reactions with zinc and sodium carbonate
  • Combination, decomposition, displacement and double displacement reactions
  • Reactivity series: Zn, Fe, Cu and Al with salt solutions
  • Electrical experiments: V–I characteristics of a resistor, series and parallel circuits
  • Light experiments: refraction through glass slabs and lens experiments
  • Biological experiments: stomata observation, CO₂ release during respiration and leaf peel mounts
  • Soap cleaning efficiency in soft and hard water
Examination & Assessment
Theory: 80 marks · 3 hours
Internal Assessment / Practical: 20 marks
Some topics may be assessed formatively only and may not contribute to year-end exam marks; students should still understand them for conceptual clarity.
